## What it's worth

Every recorded blind schedule can be settled again at ERCOT's prices and its profit put per megawatt of battery
power: times 365 for a year, times 100 for a 100 MW battery. This is a descriptive re-analysis, computed after the
results and not pre-registered, on 300 blind battery-days of 2025 and the 331 newest
([results/economics](https://huggingface.co/datasets/earakely-scale/GridSimEnv-AgentEnv/blob/main/results/economics/README.md)).

- **Training bought +\$41.2k per MW-year** [+36.6, +46.1] on 2025's days, and +\$31.2k [+22.0, +40.9] on the newest:
  from −\$14.1k, worse than leaving the battery idle, to \$27.1k, about \$4.1M a year for a 100 MW battery.
- **The trained 4B still earns less than persistence-DP**, the exact plan on yesterday's prices, a heuristic that
  costs nothing to run: −\$3.4k [−5.6, −1.5] per MW-year on 2025, and −\$2.1k [−4.4, +0.2] on the newest days, not
  separated.
- **GPT-6.1 Sol beats persistence-DP by +\$1.6k** [+0.8, +2.4] per MW-year on 2025 and +\$1.4k [+0.3, +2.7] on the
  newest days: about \$156k a year for a 100 MW battery.

### When reasoning pays

Persistence-DP is a strong rule because most days look like the day before. Splitting 2025's 191 days into quartiles
by how far each day's price shape moved from the previous day's shows where a model earns its keep.

When the day repeats yesterday, persistence-DP is within \$3.7 to \$10.5 per MW-day of the optimum: Sol stays level
with it, while Luna and the 4B lose money by moving off it. When the shape changes most, persistence-DP falls \$41.6 short;
Sol recovers \$12.7 of it [+6.5, +20.1], and Luna and the 4B only draw level. Most of Sol's edge comes from a few
days: 85% of its net gain over persistence-DP on 2025 comes from its best 19 days.

### Inference cost against value

A battery-day is one call, whatever the battery's size, so inference cost does not scale with megawatts and decision
quality does. Sol costs \$5.77 of inference per battery a year and earns about \$156k a year more than persistence-DP
for a 100 MW battery; its extra \$5.59 a year over the trained 4B buys \$497k a year of better decisions at the same
size. At grid
scale the decision, not the inference bill, decides which model to run. The case for a small trained model is cost
and control at equal quality, which the 4B reaches against GPT-6 Luna, not against Sol.

### What these dollars are and are not

- **Day-ahead energy arbitrage only**, settled at ERCOT's hourly day-ahead prices. There is no real-time market and no
  reserves, where real batteries earn much of their revenue: this is the value of the day-ahead decisions, not a
  battery's total revenue.
- **A price taker with perfect execution:** every MWh clears at the posted price and moves no price.
- **Archetype batteries**, 7.5 to 225 MW and 1 to 4 hours, with flat wear costs and no capacity fade, operating or
  capital cost.
- **Times 365 is a unit, not a forecast:** 2025's days run from January to November, and the newest pack is ten days
  of October 2026.
