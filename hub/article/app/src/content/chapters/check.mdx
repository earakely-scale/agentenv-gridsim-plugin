## The price-impact check, v5

On the ladder the market clears the agent's curves at the node's real prices, as if one battery could not move them.
v5 checks that assumption and is not a step on the ladder: the same {F.v5.n} battery-days as v3, with the same tools,
grading and models, and each day-ahead market re-cleared after the agent's own curves join ERCOT's offer stack for
that day, disclosed 60 days later. It was
[pre-registered](https://github.com/earakely-scale/agentenv-gridsim-plugin/blob/main/docs/prereg/v5-desk.md) like
the versions before it.

- **One battery moves the price by cents.** GPT-6.1 Sol's own offers moved the node's day-ahead price by
  {F.v5.impact.sol.sales} per MWh on its sales, at most {F.v5.impact.sol.max} per MWh in any hour, worth
  {F.v5.impact.sol.revenue} a battery-day: a 100–150 MW battery is a price taker, as pre-registered.
- **v5 reproduces v3.** Paired on the same battery-days, every v5 − v3 difference is within noise: Sol's P moves
  {F.v5.versus.sol.P} {F.v5.versus.sol.Pci} a battery-day and Luna's {F.v5.versus.luna.P} {F.v5.versus.luna.Pci}, and
  every interval, in P, C and R, includes zero. Two runs of the same model on the same days differ this much, while the
  price moved by cents.
- **A tie, not a new ranking.** Luna captures {F.v5.m.luna.C} and Sol {F.v5.m.sol.C}; paired, Sol minus Luna is
  {F.v5.pair.sol_luna.C} {F.v5.pair.sol_luna.Cci}, as level as in v3.
- **Where price impact would matter: a fleet desk.** A QSE trading a portfolio of batteries offers its whole fleet into
  the same day-ahead market. That desk, not one more battery, is the next step for price impact to matter.
