## The live board

Every held-out day in this article had already happened when the models played it, so in principle its prices could
have reached a model's training data. The live board removes that possibility by bidding days that have not happened
yet.

Before ERCOT's day-ahead market closes for a delivery day, at 10:00 Central the day before
[@ercot2025dam], GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, the trained 4B (run 4) and three rules (persistence-DP, ridge-DP and the
greedy rule on yesterday's prices) each **seal** a 24-hour schedule for 45 batteries: the 15 settlement points times a
1, 2 and 4-hour battery of 100 MW. A seal is a timestamped commit to the dataset's `live/` folder, made right after a
check that ERCOT has posted no prices for that day. When ERCOT posts them, every sealed schedule is settled at those
prices and the cumulative profit and loss updates.

- **Sealed means unseeable.** The prices did not exist when the schedule was published, and the commit's server
  timestamp shows it. Nothing is sent to ERCOT: these are sealed schedules for a simulated price-taking battery, not
  bids.
- **The first seal is for 29 October 2026**, published on 27 October, the day before that market closed. Passes now
  run on demand rather than daily, and a day no pass sealed in time is skipped, never filled in later.
- **It is a descriptive track record, not a test.** A handful of days gives wide intervals, and the board makes no
  claim with a verdict. Its rules were [pre-registered](https://github.com/earakely-scale/agentenv-gridsim-plugin/blob/main/docs/prereg/v1-live-board.md)
  before the first seal.
