## The real operator as the yardstick

Every desk battery-day has a real counterpart: the battery's own operator traded that day. ERCOT discloses every
resource's day-ahead bids and offers, its real-time offer curves, base points, metered output and state of charge 60
days after the operating day [@ercot_np3966; @ercot_np3965], and every evaluation day here is old enough for those
disclosures to exist. The desk uses them twice.

- **The operator's bids, replayed.** Its own curves run through the same clearing and settlement as the agent's, so
  the two are compared on the same rules and prices.
- **The operator, as settled.** Its actual awards and metered output, settled the same way.
