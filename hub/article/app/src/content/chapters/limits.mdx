## Limitations and next steps

- **DAM credit screening is not modelled.** ERCOT screens each QSE's day-ahead positions against its credit; here
  they are bounded only by the battery's megawatt limits, which favours the large day-ahead sales bought back in real
  time that carry v2's 25 January and v4's winter storm Fern.
- **A known env defect, K1, in v3, v4, the v5 check and the benchmark.** An empty reserve-offer list drew a spurious
  ASO-02 note while the energy curves in the same call were accepted and applied; there is no settlement effect, and the
  reserve figures leave those calls out of the refused offers. It is fixed on the head; the frozen runs keep it, and
  the benchmark plays v4's freeze.
- **Modelled, and labelled so.** Degradation, warranty overage, the end-of-day energy charge, capacity-short charges,
  the co-located solar's output and the desk's own price forecast are modelled. The prices, forecasts, notices, alerts
  and the operators' disclosures are ERCOT's and NWS's public records, and the market clears the agent's curves at
  those real prices, as a price taker; the v5 check re-clears the day-ahead market with the agent's offers in it.
- **One run per model per task.** The v5 check against v3 shows how far two runs of the same model on the same days
  can differ.
- **One battery is a price taker.** The check's cents are a finding about a single 100–150 MW battery, not about a
  fleet.

**Next steps.** A fleet desk, a QSE trading a portfolio of batteries into the same day-ahead market, is where price
impact can matter. And the desk is an RL environment: its reward R is ready to train on, from the benchmark's
untrained Qwen3-4B baseline, starting with a desk that always submits.

### Run it

The desk runs in process with no Docker and no key: this plays the anchor on the two pilot battery-days and writes
each one's Episode JSON and grade.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/earakely-scale/agentenv-gridsim-plugin
cd agentenv-gridsim-plugin && uv sync --extra dev
uv run python scripts/desk_run.py --pack desk-v2-pilot --policy anchor \
  --out runs/anchor
```

`--policy model` with `--base-url` plays any model behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Every battery-day, reference,
grade and Episode JSON is in the [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/earakely-scale/GridSimEnv-AgentEnv) under
`results/desk-v2` to `results/desk-v5`, and the benchmark's under `results/desk-v4-bench`. The viewer's
[benchmark page](https://earakely-scale-gridsimenv-agentenv.static.hf.space/results.html?v=bench#replays) replays every benchmark run, and its
[development versions](https://earakely-scale-gridsimenv-agentenv.static.hf.space/results.html?v=bench#ladder) every development run: the results pages for
[v2](https://earakely-scale-gridsimenv-agentenv.static.hf.space/results.html?v=v2), [v3](https://earakely-scale-gridsimenv-agentenv.static.hf.space/results.html?v=v3)
and [v4](https://earakely-scale-gridsimenv-agentenv.static.hf.space/results.html?v=v4), and for the
[v5 price-impact check](https://earakely-scale-gridsimenv-agentenv.static.hf.space/results.html?v=v5), list each task
with a replay of each model's run, and [GPT-6.1 Sol's v2 day at Battery S02](https://earakely-scale-gridsimenv-agentenv.static.hf.space/index.html?v=v2&m=sol&task=desk-v2-2026-08-23-S02) replays in 3D.
