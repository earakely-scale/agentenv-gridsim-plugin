## Limitations and next steps

- **DAM credit screening is not modelled.** ERCOT screens each QSE's day-ahead positions against its credit; here
  they are bounded only by the battery's megawatt limits, which favours the large day-ahead sales bought back in real
  time that carry v2's 25 January and v4's winter storm Fern.
- **A known env defect, K1, in v3 to v5.** An empty reserve-offer list drew a spurious ASO-02 note while the energy
  curves in the same call were accepted and applied; there is no settlement effect, and the reserve figures leave
  those calls out of the refused offers. It is fixed on the head; the frozen runs keep it.
- **Modelled, and labelled so.** Degradation, warranty overage, the end-of-day energy charge, capacity-short charges,
  the co-located solar's output and the desk's own price forecast are modelled. The prices, forecasts, notices, alerts
  and the operators' disclosures are ERCOT's and NWS's public records, and the market clears the agent's curves at
  those real prices, as a price taker until v5 re-clears the day-ahead market.
- **One run per model per task.** v5 against v3 shows how far two runs of the same model on the same days can differ.
- **One battery is a price taker.** v5's cents are a finding about a single 100–150 MW battery, not about a fleet.

**Next steps.** A portfolio desk, a QSE bidding its whole fleet of batteries into the DAM at once, is where price impact
can matter. And the desk is an RL environment: its reward R is ready to train on, starting with a desk that always
submits.

### Run it

The desk runs in process with no Docker and no key: this plays the anchor on the two pilot battery-days and writes
each one's Episode JSON and grade.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/earakely-scale/agentenv-gridsim-plugin
cd agentenv-gridsim-plugin && uv sync --extra dev
uv run python scripts/desk_run.py --pack desk-v2-pilot --policy anchor --out runs/anchor
```

`--policy model` with `--base-url` plays any model behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Every battery-day, reference,
grade and Episode JSON is in the [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/earakely-scale/GridSimEnv-AgentEnv) under
`results/desk-v2` to `results/desk-v5`, and the [viewer Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/earakely-scale/GridSimEnv-AgentEnv) replays
the desk's runs version by version.
