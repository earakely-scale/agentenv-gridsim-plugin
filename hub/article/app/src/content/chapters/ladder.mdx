## The development ladder, v2 to v4

Before the benchmark, the desk climbed a ladder of three versions on the same tools and grading, each adding one kind
of realism: v2, the live market; v3, ERCOT's reserve products and a co-located solar option; v4, three operating days
across a weather front. These are the development runs: GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna at high reasoning effort, and the
untrained Qwen3-4B with thinking off, on the desk's own runner rather than in agent harnesses. Each version was
pre-registered, piloted, frozen and then run once per model on real days at least 60 days old, at the same four real
batteries.

### v2, The Live Grid

Energy only: the day-ahead market and real time at each battery's own node, with ERCOT's notices, NWS alerts and the
battery's outages at their real minutes. {F.v2.n} battery-days on {F.v2.blocks} operating days, {F.v2.from} to
{F.v2.to}.

### v3, Sun, Storage and Reserves

ERCOT's five ancillary services in the DAM and real time, with their state-of-charge duration rules, deployments and
capacity-short charges, and a co-located solar option at some batteries (modelled). {F.v3.n} battery-days on
{F.v3.blocks} operating days.

### v4, Weather Front: the development run

Three operating days in a row across a weather front, on ERCOT's load, wind and solar forecasts as each was issued,
with three day-ahead markets to bid. {F.v4.n} three-day windows at four batteries across {F.v4.blocks} fronts;
P is summed over each window, and the CIs resample fronts. This is v4's development run on the desk's own runner, not
the benchmark: the benchmark's {F.bench.side.pre.name} half plays these same windows again, each model in its agent
harness.
