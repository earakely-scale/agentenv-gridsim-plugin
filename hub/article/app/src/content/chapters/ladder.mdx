## The ladder, v2 to v5

Each version adds one kind of realism to the same desk, tools and grading. Each was pre-registered, piloted, frozen
and then run once per model on real days at least 60 days old, at the same four real batteries.

### v2, The Live Grid

Energy only: the day-ahead market and real time at each battery's own node, with ERCOT's notices, NWS alerts and the
battery's outages at their real minutes. {F.v2.n} battery-days on {F.v2.blocks} operating days, {F.v2.from} to
{F.v2.to}.

- **Sol and Luna are level.** GPT-6.1 Sol captures {F.v2.m.sol.C} of hindsight {F.v2.m.sol.Cci} and GPT-6 Luna
  {F.v2.m.luna.C}; paired by battery-day, Sol minus Luna is {F.v2.pair.sol_luna.C} {F.v2.pair.sol_luna.Cci}.
- **One day carries the means.** On {F.v2.sens.day}, when the node's day-ahead price ran far above real time, Sol made
  {F.v2.sens.on.sol} a battery-day and the anchor {F.v2.sens.on.anchor}, from day-ahead sales bought back in real
  time: positions the DAM allows because it ignores state of charge, and which ERCOT's DAM credit screening, not
  modelled here, would limit. Without that day Sol averages {F.v2.sens.without.sol} against the operator's bids'
  {F.v2.sens.without.operator_replay}. A post-hoc check, not pre-registered.

### v3, Sun, Storage and Reserves

ERCOT's five ancillary services in the DAM and real time, with their state-of-charge duration rules, deployments and
capacity-short charges, and a co-located solar option at some batteries (modelled). {F.v3.n} battery-days on
{F.v3.blocks} operating days.

- **Still level.** Sol captures {F.v3.m.sol.C} and Luna {F.v3.m.luna.C}; paired, {F.v3.pair.sol_luna.C}

- **Reserves are where they differ.** Sol wins reserve awards on {F.v3.as.sol.awards} of {F.v3.n} battery-days and earns
  {F.v3.as.sol.lines} a battery-day from the reserve lines; Luna's reserve offers fail ERCOT's shape check
  {F.v3.as.luna.shape} times, with awards on {F.v3.as.luna.awards} of {F.v3.n}.
- **One day weighs on Sol.** On {F.v3.sens.day} Sol made more than half of its total P; without it Sol averages
  {F.v3.sens.without.sol} a battery-day. Post hoc.

### v4, Weather Front

Three operating days in a row across a weather front, on ERCOT's load, wind and solar forecasts as each was issued,
with three day-ahead markets to bid. {F.v4.n} three-day windows at four batteries across {F.v4.blocks} fronts;
P is summed over each window, and the CIs resample fronts.

- **Sol is ahead, the one clear gap.** Sol captures {F.v4.m.sol.C} {F.v4.m.sol.Cci} and Luna {F.v4.m.luna.C}; paired,
  Sol minus Luna is {F.v4.pair.sol_luna.C} {F.v4.pair.sol_luna.Cci}, the only version where that interval excludes
  zero.
- **Winter storm Fern carries the dollars.** On the front of {F.v4.sens.span}, Sol made {F.v4.sens.on.sol} a window,
  again from day-ahead sales bought back in real time, and the anchor {F.v4.sens.on.anchor}. Without Fern, Sol
  averages {F.v4.sens.without.sol} and Luna {F.v4.sens.without.luna} a window.
- **Three markets, re-bid.** Both GPT models bid each day's DAM in turn and re-bid some as forecast vintages moved.

### v5, The Market Floor

Price impact: the agent's day-ahead offers re-clear the market against the day's real offers, disclosed 60 days
later, on v3's battery-days. {F.v5.n} battery-days.

- **One battery moves the price by cents.** Sol's own offers move the node's day-ahead price by {F.v5.impact.sol.sales}
  per MWh on its sales, at most {F.v5.impact.sol.max} per MWh, worth {F.v5.impact.sol.revenue} a battery-day: a
  100–150 MW battery is a price taker, as pre-registered.
- **v5 against v3 is noise.** Paired on the same battery-days, Sol's P moves {F.v5.versus.sol.P} {F.v5.versus.sol.Pci}
  and Luna's {F.v5.versus.luna.P} {F.v5.versus.luna.Pci}; every interval includes zero. Two runs of the same model
  differ this much while the price moved by cents.
- **Luna leads in this run.** Luna captures {F.v5.m.luna.C} and Sol {F.v5.m.sol.C}; paired, Sol minus Luna is
  {F.v5.pair.sol_luna.C} {F.v5.pair.sol_luna.Cci}.
