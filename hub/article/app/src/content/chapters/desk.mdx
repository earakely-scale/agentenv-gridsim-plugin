## The desk

Everything so far is v1: the agent reads one day's prices, writes 24 numbers in one call, and an exact optimum grades
them. No battery is run that way. A real one sits behind a trading desk that bids the next day's market in the
morning, learns its awards after lunch, offers its real-time curves in the evening and watches the operating day
unfold, five minutes at a time, with ERCOT's notices, the weather and its own plant to react to. **The desk** turns
that job into the environment.

### A real battery's desk

The agent is the battery trader at a qualified scheduling entity (QSE), the registered party that trades in ERCOT's
markets on a battery's behalf. It runs one real energy storage resource (ESR) at the battery's own resource node,
from 06:00 the day before the operating day to midnight on it. The batteries are real ERCOT ESRs, shown by card
name only, with their power, energy, round-trip efficiency and standby losses calibrated from ERCOT's own disclosures
of their operation [@ercot_np3965].

Like a real desk, the agent never moves a megawatt itself. It submits ERCOT's own objects, under the rules in force
since real-time co-optimization went live in December 2025 [@ercot2025rtcb]: one energy bid/offer curve per hour,
2 to 10 price and quantity points from full charge to full discharge [@ercot_protocols], into the day-ahead market
(DAM) before its 10:00 close, real-time (RT) curves once the adjustment period opens at 18:00 the day before, and its
Current Operating Plan (COP). The market decides: the DAM clears each hour at the node's real day-ahead price (in v5,
at a re-cleared one), and in real time every 5-minute dispatch run (SCED) clears the curve in force at the node's real
price [@ercot_np6788], within the battery's limits and state of charge. The day settles on ERCOT's statement: DAM energy, RT imbalance at the node's
15-minute price and set-point deviation charges, less degradation, warranty and end-of-day energy lines that are
modelled and labelled as such.

The agent works through eleven tools, each a system a real desk uses: ERCOT's public reports (prices, ERCOT's own
load, wind and solar forecasts as issued, grid conditions and notices), plant telemetry, the desk's book, ERCOT's
market submission system with a dry-run validator that returns ERCOT-style rejection codes, and the desk's own
analytics: a price forecast (modelled, built only from data public at the time) and a co-optimiser that proposes
curves and a COP on whatever price view the agent gives it, and never submits them.

### How time works

The clock is AgentEnv's virtual clock, frozen while the agent works. Waiting means ending the turn, and there is no
`advance` tool: time moves only between turns. After every turn a post-turn hook asks the environment for its next
wake-up, takes the earlier of that and the gateway's next time trigger, moves the clock there, and has the environment
run the market forward to that minute, clearing the DAM, running every 5-minute dispatch and posting each settled
interval. The agent then wakes with the reasons. The runs in this chapter play the same desk in process; the
environment's tests check that both paths give byte-identical tool outputs and wake-ups.

- **Wake-ups are the market's.** The start, the DAM results at the minute ERCOT posted them, the adjustment period at
  18:00 and shift checkpoints at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 on the operating day; every ERCOT notice, NWS warning
  or watch, forced outage of the battery and, from v3, ancillary-service deployment, at its real minute; and any
  reminder the agent sets with `set_reminder`. The turn budget is the scarce resource: a desk that wakes for
  everything runs out.
- **Information as published.** Nothing is visible before ERCOT would have posted it: the DAM prices at their posting
  time, each 5-minute price a minute after its interval and each 15-minute settlement price two minutes after, each
  forecast vintage as issued, each notice at its minute. The real operator's bids stay hidden: ERCOT discloses them
  only 60 days later.

### Graded against the market and the real operator

The grade is the day's settled profit P. The headline is the hindsight capture C: the share of the profit over idle
that the best plan with every price known in advance would have made. Every
battery-day also carries three references, settled by the same clearing, physics and settlement code:

- **The hindsight optimum:** the best the rules allow with every real price known in advance, including DAM/RT
  spread positions, and its variant with the DAM position tied to dispatch.
- **The real operator.** ERCOT discloses every resource's day-ahead bids and offers, its real-time offer curves, base
  points, metered output and state of charge 60 days after the operating day [@ercot_np3966; @ercot_np3965]. The desk
  replays the operator's own curves through the same clearing and settlement, and settles its actual awards and
  metered output the same way. Every evaluation day is old enough for those disclosures to exist.
- **The anchor:** the desk's own optimiser, re-planning at every wake on the same forecasts the agent can read. It
  can take DAM/RT spread positions too, which pay off on scarcity days. The env's reward R, second to C and used for
  training, puts idle at 0.2 and the anchor at 1.0, with a floor when the anchor barely beats idle.

### The version ladder

Each version of the desk adds one kind of realism, as the previews did for v1, on the same desk, tools and grading:
v2, **The Live Grid**, energy only, in the DAM and real time; v3, **Sun, Storage and Reserves**, all five ERCOT
ancillary services with their state-of-charge duration rules and deployments, and a co-located solar option
(modelled); v4, **Weather Front**, three operating days across a weather front on ERCOT's forecasts as each was
issued; and v5, **The Market Floor**, where the agent's DAM offers move the price, re-cleared against the day's real
offers. Each version is pre-registered, piloted on two battery-days, then run with GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna at high
reasoning effort and the untrained Qwen3-4B, on real battery-days at least 60 days old.
